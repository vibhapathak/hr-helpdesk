# IT Equipment and Asset Policy (IT-POL-005)

Owner: IT Operations · Version 2.3 · Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Standard equipment
Every full-time employee receives a company laptop, a headset and, on request, one external monitor. Engineering roles may choose either a MacBook Pro or a Lenovo ThinkPad; all other roles receive a Dell Latitude or Lenovo ThinkPad. Hardware security keys (YubiKey) are issued on request. Company mobile phones are issued only to IT on-call staff and to employees whose role requires one, with director approval.

## 2. Laptop refresh cycle
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years from the date the device was purchased (not the date it was assigned to you). IT contacts employees about 60 days before the refresh date. Employees may request an early refresh only if the device fails a hardware diagnostic or no longer meets the minimum specification for their role, with manager approval. Peripherals such as monitors and headsets are replaced when faulty, not on a fixed cycle.

## 3. Repairs and loaner devices
Report hardware faults through the IT helpdesk. If a repair is expected to take more than 2 working days, IT issues a loaner laptop from stock. Loaner devices must be returned within 5 working days of the original device being returned from repair.

## 4. Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 4 hours of noticing the loss, so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. For theft, a police report (FIR) must be filed and a copy shared with IT within 7 days. The first loss in a 3-year period is replaced at no cost to the employee; for subsequent losses due to negligence, the employee may be charged the depreciated value of the device.

## 5. Personal use
Limited personal use of company devices is allowed provided it does not breach the Information Security Policy. Company devices may be monitored for security purposes. Family members must not use company devices.

## 6. Return of assets on exit
All company equipment must be returned on or before the last working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid courier kit. Unreturned equipment is recovered from the final settlement at the depreciated value.

## 7. Requesting equipment
New equipment and peripherals are requested through the Company Portal under "Hardware Request". Standard requests are fulfilled within 5 working days at the Noida, Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru offices.
