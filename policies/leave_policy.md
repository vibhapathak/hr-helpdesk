# Leave Policy (HR-POL-001)

Owner: Human Resources · Version 3.2 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Applies to all employees in India

## 1. Leave year and eligibility
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Full-time employees accrue leave from their date of joining. Contract employees receive a pro-rated entitlement defined in their contract (by default two-thirds of the full-time entitlement). Interns are not eligible for earned leave but receive 5 sick days and 3 casual days per internship.

## 2. Earned leave (annual leave / privilege leave)
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of earned leave per leave year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Earned leave must be applied for in the HR portal at least 7 calendar days in advance for absences of 3 days or more, and at least 1 working day in advance for shorter absences. Earned leave requires manager approval. Leave applied for but not yet approved is shown as "pending" and is deducted from the available balance until it is approved or rejected.

## 3. Carry-forward and encashment
A maximum of 10 unused earned-leave days may be carried forward into the next leave year; anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December. Carried-forward days are used first. Earned leave can be encashed only at the time of separation, up to a maximum of 30 days, at the basic salary rate. Sick and casual leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## 4. Sick leave
Full-time employees receive 10 days of sick leave per leave year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work. Sick leave may be applied for retroactively on the day of return. Unused sick leave lapses at year end.

## 5. Casual leave
Full-time employees receive 7 days of casual leave per leave year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days and cannot be combined with earned leave in the same continuous absence. Casual leave lapses at year end.

## 6. Parental leave
Birthing parents are entitled to 26 weeks (182 days) of paid maternity leave in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Non-birthing parents (including adoptive parents) are entitled to 15 working days of paid paternity/partner leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. Parental leave must be notified to HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start date where possible.

## 7. Public holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per calendar year. The location-specific holiday calendar is published in the HR portal each December. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## 8. Leave without pay
Leave without pay (LWP) may be granted for up to 30 days per leave year once all applicable paid leave is exhausted. LWP requires approval from both the manager and the HR Business Partner.

## 9. Contact
For questions about leave balances or approvals, contact the HR team via the helpdesk or at hr-helpdesk@acme-corp.example.
