# Information Security Policy (IT-POL-002)

Owner: IT Security · Version 4.1 · Effective 1 February 2026

## 1. Passwords
Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases of four or more unrelated words are recommended. Passwords must not be reused across services and must never be shared, including with IT staff — IT will never ask for your password. There is no mandatory periodic password rotation; passwords must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected or if IT Security requests it. Use of the company-provided password manager (1Password) is mandatory for storing work credentials.

## 2. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all company accounts, including email, VPN, the HR portal and source-code repositories. Approved second factors are, in order of preference: a hardware security key (YubiKey, issued by IT on request), the Okta Verify app with number matching, and TOTP codes. SMS codes are not permitted. If you lose your MFA device, contact the IT helpdesk to have it reset after identity verification by video call.

## 3. Phishing and suspicious messages
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected messages. Report suspected phishing using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook or by forwarding the message to phishing@acme-corp.example. If you entered credentials into a suspicious site, change your password immediately and contact the IT helpdesk.

## 4. VPN and network use
The corporate VPN (GlobalProtect) must be used whenever working outside a company office. Company devices must not be connected to public Wi-Fi without the VPN active. Personal devices may access email and chat only through the managed mobile apps (Intune-enrolled).

## 5. Software installation
Software must be installed through the Company Portal self-service app. Software not available there requires an IT request with business justification and is reviewed by IT Security within 3 working days. Pirated, unlicensed or personally-licensed software is prohibited on company devices. Browser extensions are restricted to the approved list.

## 6. Data classification and handling
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data (for example customer data, salary data and personal identifiers) must not be stored on personal devices, personal cloud storage or USB drives, and must not be pasted into external AI tools that are not approved by IT Security. USB mass-storage devices are blocked by default on company laptops.

## 7. Reporting security incidents
Any suspected security incident must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour of discovery via the IT helpdesk (extension 4357) or security@acme-corp.example. Incidents include malware alerts, suspected account compromise, data sent to the wrong recipient, and lost or stolen devices.
