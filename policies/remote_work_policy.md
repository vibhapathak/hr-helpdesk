# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-004)

Owner: Human Resources · Version 2.0 · Effective 1 April 2025

## 1. Hybrid work model
The default working model is hybrid: employees work from their assigned office at least 3 days per week and may work remotely for up to 2 days per week. Teams may designate fixed "anchor days" on which all team members are expected in the office. Interns are expected in the office 4 days per week.

## 2. Fully remote arrangements
Fully remote work is an exception and requires written approval from the department head and the HR Business Partner. Approvals are reviewed every 6 months. Fully remote employees must still attend in person for quarterly planning events.

## 3. Working from another city or country
Working temporarily from another city in India is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year with manager approval. Working from outside India is not permitted without prior approval from HR, Legal and IT Security because of tax, immigration and data-protection obligations; requests must be raised at least 30 days in advance.

## 4. Home workspace requirements
Employees working remotely must have a private, quiet workspace and a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps. Company devices must not be used on public Wi-Fi without the corporate VPN (see the Information Security Policy). Confidential calls must not be held in public places.

## 5. Work-from-home allowance
Employees on the hybrid or remote model receive a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR 15,000 after completing probation, claimable through the expense system with receipts. Fully remote employees additionally receive a monthly internet allowance of INR 1,500.

## 6. Availability
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Employees should be reachable on chat and email during core hours on remote days, and should set their status accurately.
